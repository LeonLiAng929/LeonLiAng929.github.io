Full papers

  1. Investigating 3D Selection Performance While Running in Extended Reality: an In-depth Study of the Effect of Speed
    Ang Li, Jarrod Knibbe, Koya Tatsuzawa, Gianluca Demartini, Stephen Viller, Geoffrey Wallis, Wolfgang Stuerzlinger, and Maxime Cordeil
    IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, 2026
    doi: 10.1109/TVCG.2026.3722681
    Abs Bib URL Video

    We explore how running speed affects target-selection performance in extended reality through a Fitts’ tapping task using direct virtual touch. A controlled study with precise motion tracking examines the effects of speed, pointing phase, and foot strike on movement time, error rate, exertion, and task load.

  2. First-Person Visualization for Sports and Physical Activities: Challenges and Opportunities
    Charles Perin, Charles-Olivier Dufresne-Camaro, Maxime Cordeil, Ut Gong, Christophe Hurter, Pourang P. Irani, Jade Kandel, Ang Li, and 5 more authors
    IEEE Computer Graphics and Applications, 2026
    Abs Bib

    We identify core visualisation and interaction challenges and research opportunities for first-person visualisation in sports and physical activities.

  3. Running with Data: a Survey of the Current Research and a Design Exploration of Future Immersive Visualisations
    Ang Li, Charles Perin, Gianluca Demartini, Stephen Viller, Jarrod Knibbe, and Maxime Cordeil
    IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics, 2025
    Abs Bib PDF URL

    This work investigates in-situ visualisations for running. We analyse 47 papers from 33 Human-Computer Interaction and Visualisation venues, identify six dimensions of a design space, and conduct workshops with 10 active runners to elicit concepts for future immersive running visualisations.

  4. Embedded and Situated Visualisation in Mixed Reality to Support Interval Running
    Ang Li, Charles Perin, Jarrod Knibbe, Gianluca Demartini, Stephen Viller, and Maxime Cordeil
    Computer Graphics Forum, 2025
    Abs Bib PDF URL Video

    We investigate mixed-reality visualisations for pace tracking during interval running. We introduce and evaluate three immersive visual designs that use the space in front of the runner and the physical environment to communicate pace.

  5. GestureExplorer: Immersive Visualisation and Exploration of Gesture Data
    Ang Li, Jiazhou Liu, Maxime Cordeil, Jack Topliss, Thammathip Piumsomboon, and Barrett Ens
    In Proceedings of the 2023 CHI Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems, 2023
    Abs Bib PDF URL Video

    GestureExplorer is an immersive analytics tool that supports interactive exploration, classification, and sensemaking with large sets of 3D temporal gesture data.
